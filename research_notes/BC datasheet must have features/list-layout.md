# Business Central list and journal layout

Scope: Microsoft Learn pages for Dynamics 365 Business Central, current as fetched on 16 October 2026. Claims below are limited to what those pages state. Analysis mode and Excel export are recorded only as places a clerk leaves the data-entry list or journal. Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations grid-footer totals were found in search and are excluded; they are a different product.

## How do column freeze, column width, show/hide columns, column order, and personalization work on a list?

### Takeaway
A user can resize columns without entering personalization mode, and can freeze, hide, show, and reorder columns only inside personalization mode. That layout is personal, stored with the user, shared across that user's views of the same list, and is not a feature Microsoft documents as copyable to another user.

### Cited Findings
- Personalization affects only the signed-in user's workspace and does not change how others work. The adjustments in the personalization article apply across the user's devices and browsers; other modifications, not detailed on that page, affect only the device and browser where they were made. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Without personalization mode, on any list page, a user can resize a column, create/rename/edit a view, bookmark the page, resize or collapse the Copilot, Help, and Page Scripting panes, hide or resize a FactBox, and expand or collapse a hierarchical list. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Column width, including in lookup dropdown lists: drag the boundary between two column headers, or double-click that boundary to autofit to a readable width. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Working with Business Central also says users can expand the width of columns and increase the height of column headers, and that a vertical freeze pane keeps some columns from scrolling on a list page or on document lines. The page does not describe the gesture for header height. — [Working with Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-product)
- Personalization mode: Settings, then Personalize. A Personalizing banner appears. If the banner says Personalize lock or Personalization blocked, the page cannot be personalized. While personalizing, Ctrl+Click navigates an action that is highlighted. Finish with Done. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Move a column by dragging it. A thick line marks a valid drop. A prohibited indicator means the element cannot be dropped there. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Hide a shown column: select the column, select the arrowhead, then Hide. Hidden fields and columns are not drawn on the page in personalization mode; find them in the Add Field to Page pane. After leaving personalization mode they disappear from view. Hidden actions are gray italic; hidden parts are shaded with diagonal lines. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Add a hidden field with + Field. The Add Field to Page pane lists hidden fields by default. Drag the field to a position, or select it so its shaded location appears and choose Show. The available fields are defined by the page developer, the source table, or a profile administrator. Users cannot create new fields. Page fields that map to the same source table are listed and moved independently. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Some added fields stay non-editable because they were designed that way or an administrator locked them. Lock editing / Unlock editing is available on a field the user previously locked; fields locked by design or by a profile admin cannot be unlocked. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Freeze pane: in personalization mode, choose the arrowhead of the column that should be the last frozen column, then Set Freeze Pane. Clear Freeze Pane on the current freeze-pane column restores the original designed position. The note on that row says you cannot remove this freeze pane. Frozen columns stay on the left while the list scrolls horizontally. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- The AL `FreezeColumn` property on a page group names one column; that column and every column before it remain in view when the user scrolls right. — [FreezeColumn property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-freezecolumn-property)
- Designer (profile customization, not end-user personalization) can also set and clear the freeze pane, adjust column width, and exclude a column from Quick Entry. Controls inside `grid` and `fixed` syntax cannot be customized or personalized in any design mode. — [Use Designer](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inclient-designer); [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Quick Entry: arrowhead on a column heading, then Exclude from Quick Entry or Include in Quick Entry, so Enter skips or stops on that column. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user); [Enter data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-data)
- Clear personalization cannot be undone. Options are Only Navigation Menu, Only Actions, Only Fields and Columns, and All. Clearing a single part clears changes inside that part only, not the part's placement or visibility. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- A personalization made on a card opened from a list appears for every record opened from that list. A change made under one Role Center is visible when the same page is opened under another Role Center. A change to a page shown in a pane applies wherever that page appears. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Personalization is unavailable while a page is in analysis mode. The Analyze switch is deactivated. Entering personalization while analysis mode is on turns analysis mode off. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- All views on a list page share one column layout: which columns are displayed, their sequence, width, freeze pane, and quick entry settings. Personalizing that layout changes every view that uses the shared layout. Some system views have a unique layout, identified when the Shared layout check box is not selected; personalizing those columns does not affect the other views. Only developers can give a unique layout to a view that started with the shared layout. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- List views are personalization stored in the database. Clearing all personalization on a list permanently removes personal views and view reordering. Views saved on a desktop browser or desktop app are available on tablet, smartphone, and in Microsoft Teams, but views cannot be modified or personalized on mobile devices. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- Microsoft documents no action that copies one user's page personalization to another user. Sharing a precise list view is explicitly unavailable. The documented substitute is to open a saved view and use Copy link; the recipient is not guaranteed an identical filter set. An unsaved filter is not included in Copy Link or in the browser URL; the filtered page must be saved as a view first. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq); [Sharing data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/across-share-data-features)
- Administrators can assign the same layout to many users by customizing a profile (role), and can override or disable users' personalization. On the Personalized Pages list, Manage then Delete clears one user's personalization of a page and returns that page to the default. User settings (role, company, and similar) are documented as separate from personalization. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user); [Customizing Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-customizing-overview); [Manage user settings and preferences](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-gb/dynamics365/business-central/admin-manage-user-settings-preferences)
- Developers and administrators can also define views for everyone or for a role. Starting with Business Central 2024 release wave 1, legacy views are no longer supported and are not displayed. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- Work date is a My Settings value, not a grid column setting. Type `t` in a date field for today's date and `w` for the work date. After a change, signing out or switching company reverts the work date to the default, so it may need to be set again. On an editable page, when the work date is not today, a reminder appears at the top with a link to My Settings and an option to dismiss it for the session; if dismissed, the work date appears in the page title. The same page also says the current work date appears in the upper-left corner of editable pages when it is not today. — [Change basic settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-change-basic-settings)
- Hierarchical lists can be set to always start expanded or collapsed with Expand All or Collapse All, including from the first column's menu. That is collapse of a hierarchy, not analysis-mode grouping. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)

### Inferences
- Freeze, width, visibility, and order are one shared list layout, while filters live in views. Changing columns on one ordinary view changes the other ordinary views of that list.
- Clear Freeze Pane restores the designed freeze column; it is not documented as a way to unfreeze every column.
- A clerk who wants colleagues to see the same columns must use a profile customization. A copied view link shares filters, not the personal column layout.

### Gaps
- The working-with-Business-Central page says column-header height can be increased, but no fetched page describes how.
- No fetched page documents a user action named Share personalization or a copy of another user's column layout. The closest official tools are profile customization, deleting a personalization, and copying a view link.
- The personalization article points to "Where is personalization stored?" for device-only changes. That page was not fetched, so which layout settings are per device remains unconfirmed.

## How do the filter pane, filter tokens, filter operators, and filter by the current cell work?

### Takeaway
The filter pane sits to the left of a primary list or worksheet, filters one field at a time with case-sensitive criteria and symbols, and can be limited to the value in the current cell. Search is a separate, case-insensitive match across visible fields.

### Cited Findings
- For lists, filters appear in a pane to the left. Open it from the dropdown arrow next to the page name, Show filter pane, or Shift+F3. The pane has three sections when they apply: Views, Filter list by, and Filter totals by. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Filter list by: + Filter, then type a field name or pick one. Filters can target any field in the table, including fields that are not visible on the page. If criteria are set on several fields, a record must match all of them. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- A column heading dropdown, Filter action, also opens the pane with that column already listed under Filter list by. A symbol in the column heading marks columns that already have a filter. Clear Filter is on that same dropdown. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Filter to the current cell: select the cell, then the dropdown arrow and Filter to This Value, or Alt+F3. The list then shows records with the same value as that cell. Shift+Alt+F3 adds the selected field to the filter pane so criteria can be typed; Ctrl+Enter returns focus from the pane to the rows. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [Keyboard quick reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts-cheatsheet)
- Ctrl+Shift+F3 opens the pane and moves focus to + Filter under Filter totals by, or closes the pane without clearing filters. Ctrl+Shift+Alt+F3, also written Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F3 on the cheat sheet, resets field and totals filters, same as Reset filters in the pane. — [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts); [Keyboard quick reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts-cheatsheet)
- Option fields filter only by selecting options in a dropdown. Selecting several options is OR. Example: Open and Released on Sales Orders shows orders that are either open or released. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Documented operators and samples: `..` interval (`1100..2100`, `..2500`, `P8..`, `22..23` for dates); `|` either/or (`1200|1300`); `<>` not equal (`<>0`, and on SQL Server `<>A*`); `>` `>=` `<` `<=`; `&` and (`>200&<1200`); `''` exact case-sensitive match, and `''` for empty text; `@` case-insensitive (`@man*`); `*` any number of unknown characters (`*Co*`, `Co*`, `*Co`); `?` one unknown character (`Hans?n`). Expressions combine, for example `5999|8100..8490` and `..1299|1400..`. A single filter with more than 200 operators is parenthesized automatically, with no change to the result. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Text intervals use lexicographic order. Filtering a Text field that stores integers with `10000..10042` also includes `100000` and `1000042`. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- If the value itself contains `&`, `(`, `)`, `=`, or `|`, wrap the expression in single quotes, for example `'J & V*'`. That quoting rule is stated only for those five symbols. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- On a numeric keypad, Alt+Decimal Separator toggles the decimal key to a period so `..` can be typed, then toggles back. The region on My Settings decides the keypad's normal character. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- Filter tokens are words replaced by the values they represent. Documented tokens: `%me` or `%user` on a user-id field such as Assigned to User ID; `%mycustomers` on a customer No. field for the My Customers list on the Role Center; `%myitems` and `%myvendors` likewise. Organizations may add custom tokens. Expressions that use only symbols such as `|` or `..` work in any language; expressions that include letters, keywords, or filter tokens work only in the language in which they were authored. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- Saving a view stores the expression, not the evaluated values. A Created Date filter of `-1W..today` still means "relative to the current date" when the view is opened next month. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- Filters and edits to predefined filters last for the session, including after leaving the page. A named view is the permanent save, using the save icon in the filter pane. Search text is not kept when leaving the page and is not stored in a view. Reset filters returns a view to its original fields and criteria. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq); [Save and personalize list views](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views)
- Views exist only on list pages. A missing Save icon can mean personalization is disabled for the role, or that the page is a worksheet. Page inspection (Ctrl+Alt+F1) shows the word List in parentheses in the Page field when the page is a list. Views are unavailable in list parts, lookups, and when a list is shown as a dialog. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- The filter pane itself is on pages whose primary content is the list, including worksheets and list pages opened from the navigation bar. It is not available for lists shown as parts (FactBoxes, Role Center parts) or as dialogs such as lookups. An embedded list, such as sales lines on a sales order, gets the filter pane when that list is focused with the focus-mode button. — [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- Show system filters appears at the bottom of the filter pane when the page has developer-defined filters, typically for context such as orders for one customer. Those filters use filter group 0. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- Search versus filter: search matches visible fields, is case-insensitive, and matches text anywhere in the field. Filtering matches one or more chosen fields exactly, including case, unless symbols are used. Hidden fields are ignored by search. Search applies only when the field's data type matches the criteria: the word today is a literal on text and code fields and a date expression on date fields, and search does not look in numeric fields. Search also does not match images, BLOB fields, FlowFilters, FlowFields, or fields that are not part of a table. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- On mobile devices or in Outlook, lists can be searched but individual fields usually cannot be filtered. In the Business Central app for Teams, both search and filter are available on lists. — [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- The same operator characters are documented for AL `SetFilter`: `..`, `&`, `|`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `<>`, `*`, `@`. — [FieldRef.SetFilter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/methods-auto/fieldref/fieldref-setfilter-method)

### Inferences
- A journal (worksheet) can be filtered with the pane and with Alt+F3, but the clerk cannot save that filter as a named view. Views are a list-page feature.
- Filter totals by changes calculated amounts; it does not add a totals footer. See the totals section.
- "Filter by the current cell" is equality to that cell's value (Filter to This Value), not a copy of whatever operator the user last typed.

### Gaps
- The filter article's date samples (`..23`, `23..`, `22..23`) are given without a separate statement of how partial dates combine with the work date. The work-date page was not linked from those samples.
- No fetched page lists every built-in filter token beyond `%me`, `%user`, `%mycustomers`, `%myitems`, and `%myvendors`. Custom tokens are acknowledged but not enumerated.

## How do sorting, multiple sorts, grouping, and the totals row work? When does BC show Sum, Count, or another total?

### Takeaway
On the data-entry list or journal, sorting is one column at a time, and there is no documented totals footer. Sum, Count, Average, Min, Max, row groups, and a last-row sum appear after the clerk leaves the page and enters analysis mode. On the list itself, Sum or Count is a per-record FlowField, and Filter totals by limits those calculations through FlowFilters.

### Cited Findings
- Sort a list by selecting the column-heading text, which switches between ascending and descending, or by the heading dropdown and the Ascending or Descending action. Alt+F7 sorts the selected column ascending or descending in list view and does not apply in tile view. Sorting is not supported for images, BLOB fields, FlowFilters, or fields that do not belong to a table. The full sort, search, and filter set is only in list view, not tile view. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts)
- Reports and XMLports can be filtered but cannot be sorted or searched. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- The list-sorting article does not describe sorting by more than one column, and it does not describe grouping rows or a totals row on the list or journal.
- Analysis mode is a separate experience. The clerk selects Enter analysis mode on a list page or query. The normal action bar is replaced by an analysis bar. Leave analysis mode returns to the page. Analysis views remain until deleted. Filters or views already set on the list control which data analysis mode shows. Analysis mode does not change the underlying data or the layout of the page when it is not in analysis mode. Some lists omit the toggle because of the `AnalysisModeEnabled` property. Version 22 required an admin to enable the feature. In version 23 and later the user needs the DATA ANALYSIS - EXEC permission set, or execute permission on system object 9640. — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- In the analysis data area, numeric columns show the sum of all values in the last row, unless row groups are defined, in which case sums appear as a subtotal for the groups. Multiple sorts: hold Shift while selecting the column headers to sort. Pin column, Pin left, keeps a column on screen while scrolling. Right-click or the column menu can also filter that column. The data area can filter totals such as sum or count. — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- The analysis summary bar, along the bottom, updates when summable cells are selected. It shows Rows (selected rows as part of available rows), Total rows (unfiltered list or query), Filtered (rows after filters), Average (selected summable fields), Count (number of selected rows), Min, Max, and Sum (sum of selected summable fields). — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- Analysis Columns pane: checkboxes show or hide columns. Row groups accept only non-numeric fields (text, date, time) and group and sum by those fields. Values accepts only numbers that can be added together, not text, date, or time, and specifies fields to sum. Pivot mode adds Column labels and is described as similar to Excel pivot tables. Date fields gain Year, Quarter, and Month hierarchy fields from the normal calendar, not a fiscal calendar; datetime fields are excluded. — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- Analysis filters use comparison operations such as Equals or Starts with and apply only to the current analysis view. Analysis views are visible only to the user who created them. Bookmarking an analysis view onto the Role Center starts in Business Central 2026 release wave 2. Adding fields from related tables starts in version 26.2 and requires the Add Related Fields permission set; before version 27.0 it also needs a feature key. Calculated fields are lost when related fields are added. Media fields are not supported in analysis mode. Personalization visibility is reflected in the Columns pane: an added field has its Show checkbox cleared until the user checks it. — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- Excel export from analysis mode is a right-click on the data area or on a selection of cells. That is another leave-the-grid action, not the journal layout. — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- On the list itself, Filter totals by appears on some lists that show calculated fields such as amounts and quantities. It adjusts dimensions that influence those calculations. The filters are controlled by FlowFilters in the page design. Open that section with Ctrl+Shift+F3. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts)
- A FlowField is calculated by `CalcFormula`. The methods are Exist, Count, Sum, Average, Min, Max, and Lookup, each with optional table filters. A FlowFilter field can be one of those filters, which is how Filter totals by changes the figure. The creating-FlowFields article shows Sum, Max, and Min examples on customer amounts using dimension and currency FlowFilters. — [CalcFormula property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-calcformula-property); [Creating FlowFields and FlowFilters](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-creating-flowfields-and-flowfilters)
- List and worksheet layout guidance places summary or extra detail for the selected row below the repeater, not in a grid footer. A well-designed list puts FactBoxes beside the list for collection statistics and for the selected row. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts)

### Inferences
- "When does the journal show Sum versus Count?" is not a journal-grid choice in the docs. Sum versus Count is chosen by the FlowField formula on a column, or, after leaving the page, by analysis mode: the last row sums numeric columns, the summary bar's Count is a count of selected rows, and the Values area is limited to fields that can be added.
- Shift-click multi-sort and row grouping are analysis-mode behaviors. Applying them means the clerk is no longer in the data-entry journal.

### Gaps
- No fetched Business Central page documents a user-toggle totals footer (Sum, Count, Average, Min, Max) on the data-entry list or journal grid. A Finance and Operations article describes that footer; it was not used.
- No fetched page documents multiple-column sort or user grouping on a worksheet or list outside analysis mode and outside collapsible hierarchy lists.
- No fetched page names the general-journal header totals (for example a balance field). Worksheet guidance only says summary fields can appear below the repeater and that header fields can hold filters or default values.

## How do FactBoxes, the page inspection pane, and the field tooltip or About this field work next to a list?

### Takeaway
FactBoxes are a right-hand pane of related parts for the current row or the whole list. Page inspection (Ctrl+Alt+F1) is a separate side pane for table, field, extension, and filter details. Field help is a tooltip on the caption, opened by pointing or by choosing the caption, plus Alt+Up Arrow while personalizing; current Learn pages describe that help as "what is this?" and do not use the words "About this field."

### Cited Findings
- A FactBox is a pane along the right side of a page with more information related to the record on the page. Collapse or expand it with the icon at the top of the pane, or Alt+F2. Resize by dragging the vertical divider; double-click the divider to reset the width. If resize is unavailable, the environment is earlier than version 26.0 or the feature Optimize screen estate usage for web is off. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- In personalization, a FactBox can be hidden and shown, and can be moved only next to other FactBoxes in the FactBox pane. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- FactBoxes can host CardPart or ListPart pages, charts, cues, and system parts such as Notes and Links. On a List page they can show information about the entire list or about the current selection; the filter passed to the FactBox decides which. `SubPageLink` ties a FactBox field to a field on the main page, so a customer list can show details for the selected customer. — [Add FactBoxes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-adding-a-factbox-to-page)
- Page structure is content, plus actions, plus FactBoxes. The FactBoxes area is a vertical list of parts. List-page design guidance calls for one or two FactBoxes for collection statistics and related details of the selected row. Card and document guidance likewise calls for one or two FactBoxes. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts)
- The filter pane is not available on a list that is itself a FactBox. — [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- Page inspection opens from Help and Support, Inspect pages and data, or Ctrl+Alt+F1, and opens on the side. It starts on the main page and updates as focus moves. Selecting a FactBox or another part highlights that area with a border, and the pane then describes that element, including the lines part on a sales order. It follows the user to other pages. — [Inspecting pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inspecting-pages); [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts)
- The pane shows the page's source table and fields, extensions that affect the page, and current filters. Page shows name, ID, and page type. Table shows source table name and ID, or the query name and ID if the page is query-based. View table opens the whole table in another browser window and requires read permission on the table plus execute permission on system object 1350 Run table. — [Inspecting pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inspecting-pages)
- Table Fields lists every source-table field for the current record, including fields not on the page: name, number, data type, PK when it is part of the key, value, extension name if an extension added it, and `Text[x]` or `Code[x]` length. Selecting a field there draws a green border around that field on the page if the page uses it. Values of blob, byte, media, and mediaset fields are not shown. Page fields not bound with SourceExp are not shown. Ctrl+C copies a value under a tab. — [Inspecting pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inspecting-pages)
- Page Filters lists UserFilters (filter pane, or AL SetFilter or SetRange), TableViewFilter (`SourceTableView`), SubFormLinkFilters (`SubPageLink` on a part), and FormViewFilters (`RunPageView` of the action that opened the page). After a search, this tab shows the exact search criteria and which fields they were applied to. — [Inspecting pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inspecting-pages); [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- From Business Central 2023 release wave 2, Explore in Visual Studio Code opens the page object and its source table. From Business Central 2026 release wave 2, an Extensions tab lists installed extensions that add or extend the page or its source table, with ID, name, version, publisher, and a derived type (Adds page, Extends page, Adds table, Extends table). Page inspection does not show extension execution time. Some details are hidden without the required permissions. Role Center inspection shows no single table until a part is selected. Report request pages show only the report name and ID. System parts such as Links or Notes, and chart parts, show limited information. — [Inspecting pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inspecting-pages)
- Tooltips appear in the client when the user points to the caption of the control. They also open when the user chooses the caption of a control or action. They answer "What is this?" and questions such as "What data can I input here?" and "What is the data used for?". Field tooltips should begin with "Specifies". Most field tooltips end with a Learn more link; action tooltips do not. Ctrl+F1 opens the learn-more content for the selected item; from 2022 release wave 1, Ctrl+F1 opens the Help pane. — [ToolTip property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-tooltip-property); [User assistance model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/user-assistance)
- List page field tooltips are shown only in the Web client and the tablet client. Tooltip text can be up to 1024 characters and is then truncated; guidance is to stay within about 200 characters. Line breaks are not rendered. — [ToolTip property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-tooltip-property); [User assistance model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/user-assistance)
- While personalizing, hover or Alt+Up Arrow shows information about a field, including fields in the Add field to page pane. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- Starting in Business Central 2024 release wave 1, a tooltip defined on a table field is inherited by pages that use the field. A tooltip defined on the page field is the one the client displays. In 2023 release wave 2 and earlier, tooltips could be defined only on page fields. — [Add tooltips](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-adding-tooltips)
- Teaching tips are separate from tooltips. They use AboutTitle and AboutText, answer "What can I do with this field or action?", and are only for the most important fields. They can be switched off on My Settings; the page title in the top left still opens the teaching tip for that page. The Help pane repeats the current page's teaching tip when one exists. — [User assistance model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/user-assistance); [Change basic settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-change-basic-settings)

### Inferences
- Next to a list, three different side surfaces exist: FactBox (business context for the row), page inspection (metadata, values, and the filters actually applied), and the tooltip (what the field means). They are not the same pane.
- The words "About this field" match the teaching-tip property names AboutTitle and AboutText only loosely. The sourced field help is the tooltip callout.

### Gaps
- No fetched current Learn page states that the tooltip callout is titled "About this field" or "About this action." The behavior of pointing at or choosing a caption is documented; the callout's title text is not.
- No fetched page describes a FactBox that shows a running total of the visible journal lines. FactBoxes are described as related information for the record or the list, with the content decided by the part and its link.

## How do search, Go to, and zoom (Ctrl+Alt+F1 or the modern equivalent) work?

### Takeaway
List search is the Search button or F3 and is not a saved filter. The modern equivalent of zoom is page inspection, opened and closed with Ctrl+Alt+F1. No current Learn page in this pass documents a list command named Go to or a command still named Zoom.

### Cited Findings
- Search is the Search button at the top of each list, or F3. The entered text may include letters, numbers, and other symbols. It tries to match all fields, is case-insensitive, and matches the beginning, middle, or end of the field. It does not match images, BLOB fields, FlowFilters, FlowFields, or fields that are not part of a table. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Search text is not kept after leaving the page and is not saved in a view. Which fields a search actually used can be read on the Page Filters tab of page inspection. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters); [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- On common pages such as Customers, Contacts, and Items, the search-field dropdown offers Use modern search and Use legacy search. This section is marked production-ready preview and subject to change. Legacy search is the method from Business Central 2024 release wave 1 and earlier and finds the exact words in the exact order. Modern search, in later releases when enabled, finds the words in any order and finds words that start with the typed letters. Quotation marks force an exact phrase. Example: `London chair` misses LONDON Swivel Chair, blue under legacy search and finds it under modern search; `hair` does not find `chair`. Modern search uses only columns a developer designates. Indexes usually update within a few seconds, but recent changes can be missing. If the menu item is absent, the feature Use optimized text search in lists is off, or the list has no designated columns. When enabled, modern search is the default. The index language can be configured for multilingual environments. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Legacy search can be narrowed with filter symbols applied across all visible fields, which the page says is less efficient than filtering a specific field. Examples: `'man'` exact case-sensitive match; `man*` starts with; `*man` ends with; `@` makes those case-insensitive. — [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- A search that runs too long shows "Searching for rows is taking too long." On-premises administrators can raise the Business Central server Search Timeout setting. — [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- Finding a page, report, or action is Tell Me, not the list search box. The working-with-Business-Central page points to Tell Me and to the Role Explorer for moving between pages. Tell Me does not list personal views; the user opens the page and then chooses the view. — [Working with Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-product); [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq)
- Ctrl+Alt+F1 opens and closes the page inspection pane, described as information about the page such as its source table, fields, filters, and extensions. The cheat sheet labels the same shortcut "Inspect page and data." The inspecting-pages article does not call this Zoom. — [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts); [Keyboard quick reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts-cheatsheet); [Inspecting pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-inspecting-pages)
- Ctrl+F12 switches between slim and wide page on the cheat sheet. That is a page-width toggle, not page inspection. — [Keyboard quick reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts-cheatsheet)

### Inferences
- For a datasheet, "search" narrows visible rows temporarily. "Go to a page" is Tell Me or Role Explorer. "Go to a record's details" is opening the card, covered in the row-cues section. None of those is documented under the name Go to.
- Ctrl+Alt+F1 is the current documented inspection shortcut. Treating it as Zoom is a naming carryover that these pages do not confirm.

### Gaps
- No fetched 2023–2026 Learn page documents a list command or shortcut named Go to.
- No fetched page says page inspection was formerly called Zoom, or documents a separate zoom control on the list.

## How do list pages differ from document subpage lines and from journals?

### Takeaway
A list is one collection, often with views and FactBoxes. A journal is a worksheet: header fields above the grid choose the batch or supply defaults, and views cannot be saved. Document lines are an embedded list part; they get a filter pane only after focus mode expands them.

### Cited Findings
- `List`: entity overviews, navigation, and inline editing of simple entities. One list with a caption. It may have field groups and subpages above and below the repeater. Columns are optimized for viewing and filtering, and for data entry when the list is editable. A summary for the selected row, if shown, goes below the list. Custom viewing options go above the list. One or two FactBoxes are recommended. The repeater takes the vertical space, reduced when a field group, card part, or list part is embedded. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts)
- `Worksheet`: line-based data entry such as journals, and inquiries. Use a worksheet instead of a list when header fields determine or subdivide the collection, so the user must choose a header value before the repeater has rows, or when header fields are defaults for data entry in the repeater. Journals are the stated example: the user selects a batch. Columns are ordered by importance. Summary fields or more detail of the selected row go below the repeater. The worksheet page type does not support the same part and group compositions as the list page type. The repeater leaves room for a group or card part above, and can also have parts below. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts)
- `Document`: one transaction. FastTabs, and the lines ListPart should immediately follow one or more header sections. The first ListPart is given extra vertical space before a scrollbar so more document lines show. A FastTab titled Lines is the recommended second section. Document pages do not put a repeater directly on the document; the repeater is inside an embedded ListPart. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts)
- `ListPart`: the embedded collection used in a document lines area or in a FactBox. A single repeater, with optional fields above or below it. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts)
- Collection pages (list and worksheet) put actions on the action bar and on the row menu, and those actions apply to the selected rows. Entity pages (card, document, list-plus) put actions on the entity named in the title. Document lines have their own actions inside the Lines section. — [Page types and layouts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-page-types-and-layouts); [Actions in the user interface](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-actions-user-interface)
- Focus mode is for a document that includes a line-items part, such as a sales order or invoice. Select the lines, then the button in the upper right, or Ctrl+Shift+F12. The lines expand to almost the whole workspace and hide the other parts of the page except the actions at the top. The filter pane for those embedded lines is available in that focused state. — [Enter data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-data); [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- A freeze pane is documented both on list pages and on document lines. — [Working with Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-product)
- Named views, the Views section, and the Save icon are list-page features. A worksheet is a stated reason the Save icon is absent. The filter pane, as distinct from saved views, is available on worksheets. — [List views FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-views-faq); [Searching and filtering FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-search-filter-faq)
- Editable lists accept a new line with Alt+N. The keyboard article's example is the G/L Budgets page. Shift+F9 posts and prints a document; Ctrl+F9 releases a document; F9 posts, issues, registers, or reverses a document. Those shortcuts are listed for list view and for tile view. — [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts)
- Opening a list from the home page is not the same as opening it from search. A searched list such as Sales Orders opens in view mode, and editing, viewing, or deleting one record is under Manage. The same list opened from the home page has no Manage action; the user opens one sales order by choosing the Number field, and cannot pin the action bar in that view. — [Working with Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-product)
- General Journal, Item Journal, Sales Journal, Purchase Journal, Cash Receipt Journal, Payment Journal, and similar pages are listed as page type Worksheet in the 2022 wave 2 action-bar inventory. Sales Lines and Purchase Lines are listed as List. That inventory is a page-type catalog, not a layout specification. — [Pages with action bar improvements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-pages-action-bar-improvements)
- List design guidance: put the most important columns first and set FreezeColumn, because the user may see only the first few columns. Actions for the current row, such as Line Comments, can be placed on the row shortcut menu. — [Designing list pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-designing-list-pages)

### Inferences
- A datasheet that models a journal should expect batch or default fields outside the grid, a single repeater, no saved views, and optional summary fields under the grid. A datasheet that models a customer list should expect views, a shared column layout, and FactBoxes. A datasheet that models order lines should expect an embedded grid that is not the whole page until focus mode.
- Personalization of columns still applies to these pages unless the page is locked. The fetched pages do not say journals are exempt from column personalization.

### Gaps
- No fetched page gives a field-by-field comparison of the general journal grid versus a document lines grid (which columns are editable, which are flowfields, whether a balance is in the header).
- The worksheet note that it "doesn't support the same part and group compositions as the List page type" is not followed by an exhaustive list of the missing compositions.

## What row cues exist: style expressions, attention indicators, flowfields, drill-down, and opening the card?

### Takeaway
Row emphasis is a per-field style, not a user setting: Attention is red italic text when a Boolean expression is true. FlowFields show a calculated figure on the row and can drill into the entries behind it. Opening the card from a list is done by choosing the number field, or by Manage when the list was opened from search. The row also has its own shortcut menu.

### Cited Findings
- The Style property formats text on a page field. It applies only when StyleExpr is true. StyleExpr may be a fixed true or false, a Boolean variable, or a Boolean expression such as "Credit Limit > Sales YTD". The default is that Style is not set. — [Style property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-style-property)
- Documented style values and their described appearance: None; Standard; StandardAccent (Blue); Strong (Bold); StrongAccent (Blue + Bold); Attention (Red + Italic); AttentionAccent (Blue + Italic); Favorable (Bold + Green); Unfavorable (Bold + Italic + Red); Ambiguous (Yellow); Subordinate (Grey). On a CueGroup the same property sets the cue's color indicator. The property applies to group, part, field, and action controls, so the color is on the control that sets it, which for a column is the field. — [Style property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-style-property)
- DrillDown on a page field is false by default. Drill-down is described as a system-wide FlowField feature that shows the transactions behind the calculated value. Example: an account balance field can open the transactions that make up the balance. — [DrillDown property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-drilldown-property)
- FlowField methods that can appear as a calculated column are Exist, Count, Sum, Average, Min, Max, and Lookup. Search does not match FlowField values. Sorting is not supported on FlowFilters. Filter totals by changes FlowField results when the formula reads a FlowFilter. — [CalcFormula property](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/properties/devenv-calcformula-property); [Sort, search, and filter](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-criteria-filters)
- Cues are a different drill-down surface, used on Role Centers rather than inside the list grid. Selecting a cue can open a list. A cue based on a FlowField uses CalcFormula; DrillDownPageId names the page that opens. — [Creating and customizing cues](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-cues-action-tiles)
- From a list opened on the home page, the user opens the individual record by choosing the Number field. Example: choose the Number field on Sales Orders to open that sales order. From a list opened through search, the list is in view mode and the user uses Manage to edit, view, or delete one entity. — [Working with Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-product)
- In analysis mode only, the column menu says the user can still peek at details about related data for each line and open the card. That is after leaving the normal list. — [Analyze list data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/analysis-mode)
- Shift+F10 opens a menu of options for the selected row in both list view and tile view. List-page design uses that row menu for actions that apply to the selected row, such as Line Comments. — [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts); [Designing list pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-designing-list-pages)
- Other documented signals near a list, not inside the cell style: a work-date reminder on editable pages when the work date is not today; mandatory-field identification is a separate topic linked from Working with Business Central; notifications can warn about events such as an overdue balance while invoicing. — [Change basic settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-change-basic-settings); [Working with Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-product)
- FactBoxes update to the selected row when their link is the current record, which is a selection cue beside the grid rather than a cue painted on the row. — [Add FactBoxes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-adding-a-factbox-to-page)

### Inferences
- Attention is not a user preference and not a whole-grid rule. A developer attaches Style and StyleExpr to each field that should turn red and italic. Several columns can carry the same expression, but the platform property is per control.
- Drill-down and opening the card are different exits from the row: drill-down shows the entries that compose a FlowField; choosing the number, or Manage, opens the entity card or document.
- A journal row can use the same style and drill-down properties because they apply to page fields. Nothing fetched says journals render an extra attention glyph beyond the Style colors.

### Gaps
- No fetched page shows the exact click target or underline treatment the web client uses for a drill-down value, or states whether drill-down works on a journal line the same way as on a customer list.
- No fetched page documents a row-level attention icon, comment marker, or error stripe as a standard list feature independent of Style.
- The mandatory-field and notifications articles were only linked, not fetched, so their exact appearance on a list or journal row is not recorded here.
