# Clerk habits that make a journal feel fast

Scope: what experienced Dynamics NAV / Business Central users, partners, and Microsoft docs say a journal or list must do so a clerk can stay on the keyboard. SAP Fiori and Salesforce are comparison only. Every claim has a URL. **Microsoft** means a Microsoft Learn or Microsoft product page. **Community** means a partner, trainer, forum, or blog. **Opinion** is marked as opinion.

BOS constraint used while judging fit: one row is one record. These notes do not recommend embedding Excel, Univer, or a formula workbook.

Sources looked for and not found in this pass: a 2022–2026 Totovic walkthrough of general or item journals, a SaaSplicator journal walkthrough, and a Use Dynamics journal walkthrough. Dynamics 365 Lab (Yun Zhu, yzhums.com) is the partner walkthrough set that did turn up.

---

## Which journal habits do trainers repeat: Quick Entry, F8, date formulas, balancing accounts, dimensions on lines, recurring journals, preview posting, and error rows?

### Takeaway

Microsoft documents all eight habits, and trainers repeat the same short list because each one removes a mouse trip or a failed post. The daily-entry set is Quick Entry, F8, a balancing account, shortcut dimensions on the line, and seeing bad rows before post. Recurring journals and date formulas are the month-end set. Preview posting is how a clerk checks the ledger impact without committing.

### Cited Findings

- **Microsoft — Quick Entry.** Enter moves only through fields marked included in Quick Entry; Shift+Enter goes backward; Ctrl+Shift+Enter leaves the list. Tab still walks every editable field in order, which Microsoft calls the disadvantage. Quick Entry is aimed at repetitive records such as a batch of sales orders or new items. Most fields start included, so the usual setup is to exclude the ones a clerk does not fill. Mandatory fields should stay included. Setup is personalization: on a list, select the column heading, then Include in Quick Entry or Exclude from Quick Entry. — [Enter data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-data); [Keyboard quick reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts-cheatsheet); [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- **Community — Quick Entry.** A Microsoft community blog lists Enter, Shift+Enter, and Ctrl+Shift+Enter next to F8 and says Quick Entry “can REALLY help with training and cutting down on errors in data entry.” That benefit claim is the author’s opinion. — [Community blog, shortcut keys and Quick Entry](https://community.dynamics.com/blogs/post/?postid=9fa50343-a6dc-ee11-a73e-6045bdb54b4d)
- **Microsoft — F8.** In an editable list only, F8 copies the field one row up in the same column into the current row. Microsoft’s own shortcut text says to follow F8 with Tab to fill line items that should match the previous row. The same shortcut is on the productivity cheat sheet as “Copy from the cell above,” grouped with Ctrl+Insert (insert a line in documents) and Ctrl+Delete (delete the line in documents, journals, and worksheets). — [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-ca/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts); [Enter data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-data); [Cheat sheet PDF](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/media/cheat_sheet.pdf)
- **Community — F8.** Ben Cole’s tip (trainer-style) repeats the same key and adds the pain it removes: repeating an account, department, or description down invoice or journal lines is faster than typing or using copy and paste. He also warns that F8 replaces a cell that already has data. The community shortcut blog repeats F8 as item 1. — [Ben Cole, F8](https://www.bencolebc.com/post/bc-tip-5-f8-copy-data-from-cell-above); [Community blog](https://community.dynamics.com/blogs/post/?postid=9fa50343-a6dc-ee11-a73e-6045bdb54b4d)
- **Microsoft — date formulas.** A date formula is a short letter-and-number expression, at most 20 characters. Units are C (current), D, W, M, Q, Y. Examples Microsoft gives: CW, CM (last day of the month), 10D, D10 (next 10th of a month), WD4, CM+10D, -1Y. On recurring journal lines, Recurring Frequency is a date formula and must be filled. Microsoft’s worked examples: `1M` keeps the same day next month; `1D+1M-1D` lands on the last day of each month regardless of month length; `1M+CM` is one full month plus the rest of the current month. A blank Reversal Date Calculation posts the reversing line the next day; `5D` posts it five days out. That reversal field is hidden until the user personalizes it onto the page. Date formulas are also used for Allow Posting From/To, evaluated against the user’s work date (example: `-CM` to `CM` is the current month). — [Entering dates and times](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-date-ranges); [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals); [Specify posting periods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/finance-how-specify-posting-periods)
- **Community — date formulas on recurring lines.** Kwixand restates the same `1M`, `1D+1M-1D`, and `1M+CM` examples and the rule that Recurring Frequency must be filled. It does not add a different formula set. — [Kwixand, recurring journals](https://www.kwixand.com/post/using-recurring-reversing-recurring-journals-in-d365-business-central)
- **Microsoft — balancing accounts.** If the journal batch has a default balancing account, it fills in when the clerk enters Account No. Otherwise both Account No. and Bal. Account No. are typed. A positive amount debits the main account and credits the balancing account; a negative amount does the opposite. VAT is calculated separately for the two sides, so they can carry different rates. Suggest Balancing Amount, a checkbox on the batch, prefills Amount on lines that share a document number with the value needed to balance that document. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Community — balancing accounts.** Yun Zhu (Dynamics 365 Lab) walks the same two patterns and adds an observation: users in Asia, as far as he knows, prefer a second journal line over Bal. Account No., and on that pattern the second Amount is manual unless Suggest Balancing Amount is on. He shows the amount being filled before the line is inserted. The regional preference is his observation, not a Microsoft statistic. — [Dynamics 365 Lab, Suggest Balancing Amount](https://yzhums.com/54917/)
- **Microsoft — dimensions on the line.** A journal line stores one dimension set ID, not a column per dimension. Two global dimensions and up to eight shortcut dimensions can still be fields on the journal line, document line, and ledger entry, so the clerk does not open the Dimensions page for those. Global dimensions are the ones used as filters on reports and batch jobs; choose them carefully because changing them rewrites posted entries, can lock tables, and can sign the user out while Business Central prepares the new dimension. Default dimensions on an account are copied onto the line when the account number is entered, and can be overwritten. Code Mandatory with a blank value means the dimension is required but has no default. — [Work with dimensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/finance-dimensions)
- **Microsoft — recurring journals.** A recurring journal keeps accounts, dimensions, and dimension values after posting, for transactions that barely change (rent, subscriptions, electricity, heat). Recurring Method controls the amount: F Fixed keeps it; V Variable clears it; B Balance allocates and zeros the account; RF/RV/RB also post a balancing entry the next day; BD and RBD allocate a G/L balance by dimension. Allocations are entered once, stay after posting, and “act as balancing account line.” Allocation % calculates the amount with the opposite sign. You cannot use allocation accounts on a batch that has an approval workflow for creating journal lines. Putting the same dimension value on both the recurring line and the allocation line makes the posted dimensions wrong. Expiration Date stops posting after that date without deleting the line; blank means the line posts until someone deletes it. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals); [Allocation keys](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-how-use-allocation-keys-general-journals); [Recurring method enum](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/application/base-application/enum/microsoft.finance.generalledger.journal.gen.-journal-recurring-method)
- **Community — recurring vs standard journals.** Yun Zhu documents Save as Standard Journal and Get Standard Journals and writes, as his opinion, that standard journals are easier than recurring journals and save re-entry. Microsoft documents the same save/get actions for item journals and says the steps also apply to general journals, with the caveat that a standard journal may not carry every field into the ledger entry. — [Dynamics 365 Lab, standard journals](https://yzhums.com/15496/); [General journals, standard journals section](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Microsoft — preview posting.** On every document and journal that can be posted, Preview Posting shows the kinds of entries posting would create. The purchase-invoice walkthrough selects G/L Entry and then Show Related Entries. The preview codeunit runs posting “without database commits.” — [Preview entries before you post](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-how-preview-post-results); [Gen. Jnl.-Post Preview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/application/base-application/codeunit/microsoft.finance.generalledger.preview.gen.-jnl.-post-preview)
- **Community — extended preview.** A community write-up of the BC v19 change says General Ledger Setup has Posting Preview Type = Standard or Extended. Extended groups G/L entries, VAT entries, and related entries (including extension entries) and can show a hierarchical view by account. The author says journal preview looks and feels like document preview. That layout detail is community description of the product, not the short Learn article. — [Community, new posting preview type](https://community.dynamics.com/blogs/post/?postid=5a0cfef5-9cf0-4257-b8f2-a1b3058789e8)
- **Microsoft — error rows.** Background Error Check on the journal batch validates finance journals (general or payment) while the clerk works, “to help prevent delays when posting.” On load, and again when the clerk chooses another line, the Check Document or Journal Check FactBox lists issues on the current line and the whole batch. The Issues total tile opens the overview. Show Lines with Issues and Show All Lines toggle the grid. Journal Line Details FactBox shows the G/L account, customer, or vendor and the posting setup for the current line. Admins can also enable real-time check on My Notifications: Enable Data Check for journals, Show the Document Check FactBox for documents. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Microsoft — document numbers, a related habit trainers hit in the same breath.** If the batch has a number series, document numbers on lines or groups must be sequential or posting fails. Renumber Document Numbers fixes them and keeps lines that share a number grouped. It also updates Applies-to ID and Applies-to Doc. No. The action works on a filtered view. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Microsoft — Show More / Show Fewer Columns.** Fewer columns hide fields such as Account Type and use the same posting date for every line. More columns are required when one journal entry needs several posting dates. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)

### Inferences

- The repeated habit is not “more columns.” It is a short keyboard path over the columns that change, plus a way to see that the batch will post.
- Date formulas belong on date fields of a record (recurring frequency, reversal date, allowed posting window). They are not a reason to put a spreadsheet in the journal.
- Suggest Balancing Amount matters when clerks balance with a second line instead of Bal. Account No. Both patterns are documented; the second-line preference is community observation.

### Gaps

- No Totovic, SaaSplicator, or Use Dynamics article from 2022–2026 was retrieved, so this section cannot claim those trainers repeat the same list.
- Microsoft’s preview article demonstrates a purchase invoice, not a general journal screenshot. Journal support is stated (“every document and journal”) and the preview codeunit names general journals; the extended three-group layout is community-described.
- No Microsoft page in this pass says how many journal lines a clerk is expected to enter per minute, or ranks the eight habits.

## What do users complain is slow or easy to get wrong on BC journals and lists?

### Takeaway

The documented pains are a post that stops on the first error, document numbers out of series, one posting date hiding in Fewer Columns, and dimension or VAT rules that fail only at post. The speed complaints that have URLs are about posting tens of thousands of lines and about Account No. validation when workflows run, not about scrolling a short batch.

### Cited Findings

- **Microsoft — the delay the error FactBox is meant to remove.** Background Error Check exists so a mistake does not show up as a delay at post time. The FactBox updates when the batch loads and when the clerk moves to another line. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Community — posting used to reveal one error at a time.** A Rand Group article, as returned in search, says the old behavior was that posting stopped at the first error and showed only that error, so a long journal was posted again and again, which was worse when one person entered and another posted. It attributes the 2020 wave 2 journal-check feature to telemetry from thousands of users. A later fetch of that same URL returned the firm’s marketing homepage, not the article body, so treat the “first error only” history as a search extract of that URL, not as a re-read of the page. — [Rand Group, how to check financial journals](https://www.randgroup.com/insights/microsoft/dynamics-365/business-central/how-to-check-financial-journals-in-business-central/)
- **Community — large journals post very slowly.** On Business Central 26, one forum report says about 77,000 general journal lines took almost five days, with no custom apps, and with analysis views and Copilot disabled. Yun Zhu’s reply does not confirm a version-26 regression; he asks whether 1–2 lines are slow and points at the in-client performance profiler. — [Forum, performance posting large journals](https://community.dynamics.com/forums/thread/details/?threadid=41e86cf6-c23a-f011-b4cc-000d3a990cc2)
- **Community — typing the account feels slow.** A Microsoft Q&A report says General Journal Line pages respond very slowly when Account No. or Account Type is validated, traced in the debugger to `FindWorkflowStepInstanceWithOptionalWorkflowStart` in workflow management, with workflows enabled and custom extensions uninstalled. The published reply does not confirm a product bug; it asks for a performance profile and a support request. — [Microsoft Q&A, journal validation performance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5904672/365-business-central-issue-performance-issue-when)
- **Community — preview posting can fail on permissions.** A Power Platform community article says general-journal preview posting can error when the user has Read but not Modify on General Ledger Setup. — [Preview posting issues](https://community.powerplatform.com/article/KA-24120)
- **Community — document numbers are easy to get wrong.** Gavin Whittaker (The Dynamics Explorer, November 2025) shows Preview Post succeeding and Post failing with “You have one or more documents that must be posted before you post document no.… according to your company’s No. Series setup” because the batch number series had Manual Nos. off while the clerk typed a document number. His fixes are turn Manual Nos. on, clear the number series, or use Renumber Document Numbers. — [The Dynamics Explorer, journal number series](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2025/11/10/dynamics-365-business-central-error-message-you-have-one-or-more-documents-that-must-be-posted-before-you-post-document-no-when-posting-a-journal/)
- **Community — lines that share a document number must agree.** Forum replies say lines with the same Document No. need the same document type, must not mix customers or vendors, and need a customer or vendor when VAT business and product posting groups are set. That is community explanation of a posting error, not a Learn article retrieved here. — [Forum, VAT and document number](https://community.dynamics.com/forums/thread/details/?threadid=67d4f023-256d-f011-bec2-7ced8d1c3ddd)
- **Microsoft — Fewer Columns hides a date mistake.** With fewer columns, every line uses the same posting date. A clerk who needs several dates must show more columns. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Microsoft — dimensions are easy to set up in a way that hurts later.** Changing a global dimension updates posted entries, may lock tables, and may not finish in one transaction on a large database. Blocked dimension combinations cannot be posted together. Default dimension priorities exist because customer, item, and location can propose different values; Microsoft’s own example is a conflict that asks whether to overwrite lines, and it says to test the order in which people enter data. Adjust Exchange Rates and Post Inventory Cost to G/L cannot use Code Mandatory or Same Code. — [Work with dimensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/finance-dimensions)
- **Community — F8 overwrites.** Ben Cole’s warning: F8 replaces the current cell with the cell above. — [Ben Cole, F8](https://www.bencolebc.com/post/bc-tip-5-f8-copy-data-from-cell-above)
- **Community — a blank description is a common audit miss.** Yun Zhu shows Omit Default Descr. in Jnl. on the G/L account card (a Netherlands localization that also exists in W1). If it is on, the journal does not copy the account name into Description, and posting errors with “Description must have a value.” He notes you still cannot force the description to be detailed. — [Dynamics 365 Lab, omit default description](https://yzhums.com/67275/)
- **Microsoft — standard journals can drop fields.** Reusing a standard journal can produce a general journal with less information than typing it, and fields such as Payment Method Code may not exist on the resulting ledger entries. Changes made while showing the standard journal are saved immediately. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)

### Inferences

- “Feels slow” in clerk language usually means either the account field hesitates on every line, or post is the first time the batch is checked. The FactBox and preview are Microsoft’s answers to the second. The first is an open support-style report, not a documented design limit.
- The easy mistakes are structural: shared document number, hidden posting date, VAT without a customer or vendor, and a number series that does not allow the number the clerk typed. A grid that waits until post to say so recreates the pain Background Error Check was added to remove.

### Gaps

- No retrieved source gives a measured time for ordinary journal entry (tens of lines) on a healthy tenant.
- No retrieved source says the background error check itself makes typing slower.
- The Rand Group historical claim is only as strong as the search extract; the article body was not re-read successfully.
- Josh Knox’s December 2022 “journal entry” hotkey post is about the Dynamics 365 Finance general-ledger journal (Alt+N, journal-name dropdown), not Business Central. It is not used as BC evidence. — [iamJoshKnox](https://iamjoshknox.com/2022/12/23/hotkey-highlights-and-happy-holidays-tips-for-efficient-journal-entry/)

## How do dimensions, comments, and line attachments show without leaving the line?

### Takeaway

Microsoft keeps the dimensions clerks use most often as columns on the line, and keeps account context in a FactBox for the current line. Everything else — the rest of the dimension set, formal comment sheets, and files — is a related page or a FactBox the clerk opens beside the record. Standard Business Central does not show a full dimension set, a comment thread, and files inside the journal cell.

### Cited Findings

- **Microsoft — dimensions without opening Dimensions.** “On journal and document lines, you can select global and shortcut dimensions in a field on the line.” Two global and eight shortcut dimensions. The full combination is still one dimension set ID on the line. On a sales order, Microsoft’s own pattern is shortcut dimensions on the line and the Dimensions button for the rest. It says a journal works the same way if shortcut dimensions are fields on the lines. On ledger entries, the Ledger Entry Dimensions page follows the selected entry as you scroll, one entry at a time. — [Work with dimensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/finance-dimensions)
- **Microsoft — line context beside the grid.** Journal Line Details FactBox gives “quick overview and access to data from journal lines, such as the G/L account, customer, or vendor, and the posting setup.” The journal-check FactBox lists issues for the current line and the batch. Users can collapse or resize the FactBox pane. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals); [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- **Microsoft — links on the journal survive posting.** “When you create a journal, you can add links that give context to its transactions. When you post the journal, Business Central copies the links to the posted journal and ledger entries.” The example is receipt images on SharePoint for an auditor. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Microsoft — attachments, links, and notes live on the record’s FactBox, not in the cell.** On most list pages, cards, and documents, the Attachments tab of the FactBox shows a count of files, links, and notes. They stay attached as a document moves to a later state (sales order to posted invoice) and are not printed. Notes there are not workflow notes. A developer article says a FactBox shows related information for the current record and can host Notes and Links system parts. — [Add attachments, links, and notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-how-add-link-to-record); [Add FactBoxes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/developer/devenv-adding-a-factbox-to-page)
- **Microsoft — line-level files on documents use the lines, not a second grid product.** The external-storage article says users still attach from the Attachments FactBox, “or the Attachments action on the Lines FastTab for line-level attachments,” and gives the sales order as the example. Drag and drop onto the FactBox is described for that record. — [Store document attachments externally](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/across-store-document-attachments-externally)
- **Microsoft — journal lines have an API relation for both.** The `journalLine` API navigation includes `attachments` and `dimensionSetLines`. — [journalLine resource](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/api-reference/v2.0/resources/dynamics_journalline)
- **Community — the journal Comment column is a field, and people disagree about whether it lasts.** Forum replies identify Gen. Journal Line (table 81) field 5618 Comment, and also point at Comment Line (table 97) with Table Name = Gen. Journal Line. One reply says the on-screen comment is a temporary UI note and is not carried to ledger entries. Another says the API exposes it as a user-specified comment and warns not to assume it is available after posting unless you confirm the path you need. Comment Line itself is a separate table (date, code, comment text of 80 characters). — [Forum, journal comment field](https://community.dynamics.com/forums/thread/details/?threadid=ce9811fe-03a3-f011-bbd3-7ced8d1e92e2); [Table Comment Line](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/application/base-application/table/microsoft.foundation.comment.comment-line)
- **Community — comment lines on sales documents are extra lines, not a side note on the item line.** Steve Endow (2023) describes Type = Comment on a sales line, with Description limited to 100 characters, plus Standard Text Codes and Extended Text that insert more comment lines. That is a document-line pattern, not a general-journal FactBox. — [Steve Endow, comment lines](https://blog.steveendow.com/2023/06/business-central-simple-tip-5-comment.html)
- **Community — a line-comment FactBox is an add-on.** Apportunix Convenient Comments adds a Line Comments FactBox “that shows the comments of the currently selected document line” on pages such as the sales order. That is not base Business Central. — [Apportunix comment FactBoxes](https://docs.convenientcomments.apportunix.com/Comment-Factboxes.html)
- **Community — item journal line files are a customization.** Dharmendra’s September 2025 post says the standard Document Attachment table does not store the template name and batch name needed to identify an item journal line, and shows a page extension that puts Doc. Attachment List Factbox on the item journal keyed by template, batch, and line number. Yun Zhu similarly shows that dropping the attachment FactBox on an arbitrary page does not by itself write the table link. — [Item journal attachments](https://dharmendra-d365.blogspot.com/2025/09/document-attachment-with-item-journal.html); [Dynamics 365 Lab, attachments FactBox](https://yzhums.com/11177/)
- **Community — links and notes on a list follow the selected row.** Yun Zhu writes that for links and notes you can do this on the list page by first selecting the related line, then opening the Attachments tab. — [Dynamics 365 Lab, attachments FactBox](https://yzhums.com/11177/)

### Inferences

- The base pattern that already feels like “don’t leave the line” is: put the frequent dimensions in columns, and let a side pane follow the selected row for account details, issues, links, and notes.
- A comment the auditor must find after posting should be something Microsoft already copies (the journal link, or the description), not the journal Comment field, until persistence is confirmed. The forum contradicts itself on that field.
- Line file attachments on journals are not a habit clerks get for free on every journal. They are standard on document lines such as sales orders, and a partner extension on item journal lines.

### Gaps

- No Microsoft Learn page retrieved here shows a general-journal screenshot with a per-line attachment FactBox that updates as the clerk moves rows.
- No source states a character limit for journal field 5618 in a Microsoft doc read in full. Community posts say Text[250] and the API comment property; Comment Line.Comment is Text[80] on the Microsoft table page.
- Whether general-journal links are per line or per batch is not spelled out beyond “links that give context to its transactions” and the copy to posted journal and ledger entries.

## What does SAP enjoy (F4 value help, table control personalization) and Salesforce (inline edit, mass edit) add that a BC-style sheet should still consider?

### Takeaway

Business Central already has an editable journal grid, a lookup on the field, and per-user column personalization including a freeze pane. SAP’s extra is a real value-help dialog: search by several attributes, ranges, and a hit list that shows more than the code. Salesforce’s extra is editing existing rows in place and writing one value onto many selected records. A record grid can take both ideas without becoming a workbook.

### Cited Findings

- **SAP, documented — F4 is input help, F1 is field help.** On an SAP GUI input field, F4 opens help; if the field has F4 help, an icon appears when the field is focused. In more complex applications the dialog offers selection options. Backspace with the cursor at the start of the field opens a history of previous entries. F1 opens the field documentation. — [SAP GUI input field](https://help.sap.com/doc/saphelp_nw73ehp1/7.31.19/en-US/48/159c9b88474c23e10000000a42189b/content.htm?no_cache=true); [SAP Learning, F1 and F4](https://learning.sap.com/courses/business-processes-in-sap-hcm-on-s-4hana/finding-help)
- **SAP, documented — search helps are more than a dropdown.** The ABAP Dictionary describes input help as a standard function: the user sees possible values, which can include further columns when the field is a formal key. The standard process can offer several search paths; each path has restrictions in a dialog before the hit list. An elementary search help is one path; a collective search help combines several. — [Search helps](https://help.sap.com/doc/saphelp_snc700_ehp01/7.0.1/en-US/cf/21ee2b446011d189700000e8322d00/content.htm?no_cache=true)
- **SAP, documented — Fiori value help is a dialog with filters, a table, and conditions.** The design-system page says to use the value-help dialog when the user must search by attributes such as city and company, when the set is large, or when the user must select ranges and exclusions. One page says “more than 200 items.” The Fiori elements developer page says “for example, more than 1000 items.” Those thresholds conflict; both are SAP docs. The dialog can load immediately or wait until the user chooses Go. The list can be single or multiple selection; Define Conditions supports include and exclude ranges. The help icon on the same business object’s ID field inside the dialog opens only conditions, to avoid a loop. — [Value help dialog usage](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-84/ui-elements/value-help-dialog/usage); [Fiori elements value help](https://github.com/SAP-docs/sapui5/blob/main/docs/06_SAP_Fiori_Elements/value-help-dialog-3faed83.md)
- **SAP, documented — type-ahead exists, details are paywalled.** SAP KBA 2294815 says type-ahead search in F4 is available from SAP NetWeaver 7.40 SP03 and SAP GUI 7.30 patch 5. The public page is a preview; the full article needs a login. — [KBA 2294815](https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2294815)
- **SAP, documented — table personalization.** Fiori elements turns table personalization on by default: add or remove columns, filter, sort, and group. A manifest setting can turn each of those off. The older design-system personalization dialog is opened from a Personalize button and supports show or hide all, live search of column names, move up and down, and undo back to the initial layout. — [Enabling table personalization](https://github.com/SAP-docs/sapui5/blob/main/docs/06_SAP_Fiori_Elements/enabling-table-personalization-3e2b4d2.md); [Table personalization dialog](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-84/ui-elements/table-personalization-dialog/usage)
- **Microsoft — BC already personalizes the grid per user.** Personalization does not change anyone else’s workspace. On a list, without entering personalization mode, a user can resize a column by dragging or by double-clicking the boundary (including lookup dropdowns), hide or resize the FactBox, and expand or collapse a hierarchy. In personalization mode the user can move, hide, show, lock editing, set or clear the freeze pane (columns that stay on the left while scrolling horizontally), and include or exclude Quick Entry. Hidden fields are added from a field pane; the user cannot invent fields. Personalization cannot be cleared in part. Analysis mode and personalization mode are mutually exclusive. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- **Salesforce, documented — inline edit on a list view.** Salesforce Help (Japanese article retrieved; the English URL of the same id returned only a loading shell) says not every field can be edited in the list: a pencil marks an editable cell and a lock marks one that cannot. Inline edit is unavailable when the list contains more than one record type; filter to one type first. Edit the cell, then Save. If two people edit the same field, only the latest edit is saved. The article says the same considerations apply when editing records in the multi-line layout. — [Salesforce Help, inline edit in Lightning list views](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=xcloud.customviews_edit_inline_listview_lex.htm&language=ja&type=5)
- **Community — mass edit writes one value to the selected rows.** Forvis Mazars (November 2024) says to filter a list view to the records you want, and if the org uses record types, filter to exactly one. Enhanced Lists and Inline Editing must be on. Hover shows a pencil or a lock. Check several rows, double-click the field, choose to apply the change to the selected records, and save. An older Salesforce Thinkers walkthrough (2019) shows the dialog choice “The record clicked” versus “All 20 selected records,” and says the Mass Edits from Lists permission is required, that inline edit is off when Accessibility Mode is on, and that filter logic / OR criteria and lookup-filter fields block it. A 2017 Salesforce admins post says Lightning list views could update up to 200 records. That 200 figure is from 2017; this pass did not confirm the current limit. — [Forvis Mazars, mass update from list views](https://www.forvismazars.us/forsights/2024/11/update-multiple-records-with-list-views-in-salesforce); [Salesforce Thinkers](https://salesforcethinkers.home.blog/2019/09/10/inline-editing-in-salesforce-mass-edit-records/); [Salesforce admins, Winter ’18](https://admin.salesforce.com/blog/2017/winter-18-release-overview-admins); [Stack Exchange, inline-edit constraints](https://salesforce.stackexchange.com/questions/98934/mass-edit-option-unable-to-see-the-lock-and-pencil-icons-on-the-fields)
- **Salesforce, documented — some mass-update help is still Classic-oriented.** A short Salesforce Help article says to use a list view in Salesforce Classic to select multiple records and edit with inline editing, and points users who cannot mass edit to a further article. — [Mass update records](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000382519&language=en_US&type=1)

### Inferences

- **Opinion.** The SAP behavior worth copying onto a BC-style sheet is the lookup, not the GUI key name. Clerks expect to search an account by name and code, see extra columns, and pick without leaving the row. Range and exclude conditions matter on filters more than on a journal account cell.
- **Opinion.** BC personalization already covers SAP’s column show, hide, order, and freeze. Grouping a transaction journal is a report feature, not a data-entry feature.
- **Opinion.** Salesforce inline edit is how BC journals already work: the row is the record. The piece BC’s F8 does not cover is multi-select “set this column to the same value on the ticked rows.” That is still one field on many records, not a formula grid.
- Classic SAP “table control” personalization (the GUI table-settings button) was not in the pages retrieved. The Fiori personalization docs are the modern documented equivalent.

### Gaps

- No page in this pass shows a BC user stating that they miss SAP F4 or Salesforce mass edit by name. The comparison is capability, not a survey.
- Current Salesforce mass-edit row limit after 2017 was not confirmed.
- SAP’s “more than 200” and “more than 1000” value-help thresholds disagree; neither is a measured BC requirement.

## Which features are nice-to-have rather than required for a first production journal?

### Takeaway

**Opinion, grounded in how often Microsoft and trainers put the habit on the entry path:** a first production journal needs a short keyboard path, a lookup, a balancing rule, the frequent dimensions on the line, visible errors, and a preview that does not post. Recurring journals, allocations, standard journals, extended preview, mass edit, and line files are real product features that can wait until a clerk repeats the same entry every month or must attach a receipt per line.

### Cited Findings

- **Microsoft — on the first-use path.** Quick Entry, F8, Ctrl+Delete on journal lines, default or manual balancing accounts, Suggest Balancing Amount, shortcut dimensions as line fields, Background Error Check with Show Lines with Issues, Preview Posting, and Renumber Document Numbers are all documented as part of entering or posting a general journal, not as optional analysis. — [Enter data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-enter-data); [Keyboard shortcuts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-ca/dynamics365/business-central/keyboard-shortcuts); [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals); [Dimensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/finance-dimensions); [Preview posting](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-how-preview-post-results)
- **Microsoft — present, but for a repeated entry rather than the first one.** Recurring methods, frequency formulas, allocations, and reversal dates are documented for rent, subscriptions, and accruals. Standard journals exist so a clerk can save lines they “know you’re likely to create again later.” Copy Selected Lines to Journal and Copy G/L Register to Journal, including opposite signs, are on the Posted General Journal page for corrections, and copying to posted journal lines is a template or batch setting that can be turned off. — [General journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-work-general-journals)
- **Community opinion — standard journals can replace recurring journals for some clerks.** Yun Zhu writes that he personally finds standard journals easier than recurring journals. That is one partner’s opinion. — [Dynamics 365 Lab, standard journals](https://yzhums.com/15496/)
- **Microsoft — personalization is expected, and it is per user.** Freeze pane, column resize, hide column, and Quick Entry exclusion are documented user personalization, not posting rules. — [Personalize your workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/ui-personalization-user)
- **Community — description enforcement and line files are local or extended.** Omit Default Descr. in Jnl. is a Netherlands feature also in W1. Item-journal file attachments are shown as a customization. — [Dynamics 365 Lab, descriptions](https://yzhums.com/67275/); [Item journal attachments](https://dharmendra-d365.blogspot.com/2025/09/document-attachment-with-item-journal.html)

### Inferences

**Opinion. Required for a first production journal (the pain each one removes):**

| Behavior | Pain removed |
| --- | --- |
| Quick Entry so Enter skips columns the clerk does not fill | Tab through posting groups and fields that already defaulted |
| F8 copy-down, without wiping a cell the clerk did not mean to replace | Retyping the same account, date, or dimension down the batch |
| Insert and delete a line from the keyboard | Mouse trips to add or remove a row |
| Lookup on the row: code, name, and a short hit list | Opening a card to find the account |
| Balancing account and/or suggest the amount that ties a document number | A batch that will not post because the two sides do not match |
| Shortcut dimensions as columns; other dimensions in a pane that follows the row | Leaving the line for department or project on every row, or building a G/L account per department |
| Show lines with issues, for the line and the batch, before post | Discovering the first error only when posting, then posting again |
| Preview the entries posting would create, without committing | Posting in order to see whether the accounts and VAT are right |
| Document numbers kept in series, with a renumber action | “Documents that must be posted before…” after the clerk typed a number |
| Posting date visible when lines do not share one date | Fewer Columns silently giving every line the same date |

**Opinion. Nice-to-have, not required to post the first real journal:**

- Recurring methods, allocation percentages, balance-by-dimension, and reversal date formulas. They remove monthly re-entry. A clerk who posts a one-off batch does not need them on day one.
- Save as standard journal / get standard journal. Same reason. Yun Zhu prefers them to recurring journals; either can wait.
- Extended posting preview with hierarchical G/L grouping. Standard preview already shows the entries.
- Salesforce-style mass edit of many existing rows to one value. Useful later. F8 covers the new-line case one cell at a time.
- SAP condition and range tabs inside account lookup, and grouping rows in the entry grid.
- Per-line file attachments and a comment sheet that follows the row. A link on the journal that is copied to the posted entries is the documented audit trail. Line files are standard on some documents and custom on item journals.
- Freeze pane, column width, and focus mode (Ctrl+Shift+F12 on the cheat sheet). They make a wide list calmer. They do not decide whether the journal posts.
- Forcing a blank description so the clerk must type one. Local practice, not the base journal.
- Copy a posted journal back with opposite signs. That is correction, after the first journal already posted.

**Opinion. Do not add, because it fights one row = one record:** an embedded workbook, Excel-style formula chains across rows, or Univer. Date formulas in BC are a value in a date field (`1M`, `CM`, `1D+1M-1D`), calculated by the system, not a cell formula the clerk maintains.

### Gaps

- No trainer source retrieved here publishes an explicit “version 1 / later” cut. The split above is opinion inferred from which behaviors Microsoft puts on the general journal entry page versus which it documents for recurring, posted, or localized cases.
- Focus mode is on the Microsoft cheat sheet; this pass did not retrieve a Learn article that tells clerks to use it on journals, so it stays in the polish group.
